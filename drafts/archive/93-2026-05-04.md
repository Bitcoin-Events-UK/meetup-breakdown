####  Tuesday

🍺 [**Canterbury Bitcoin**](https://www.meetup.com/Canterbury-Bitcoin-Meetup-Group): ⚠️ **Venue for May TBC** — previous venue was Millers Arms, Canterbury at 19:00. Check the Meetup page for confirmed details.

A social gathering for anyone interested in Bitcoin — beginners and OGs equally welcome. The John Hewitt has a great Bitcoin ethos and accepts bitcoin at the bar.

####  Friday

☕️ [**Newcastle Bitcoin Coffee Morning Meetup**](https://meetu.ps/e/PqtTh/v822r/i): Every Friday morning at [**Pumphreys Coffee Centre and Brewing Emporium**](https://pumphreys-coffee.co.uk/pumphreys-coffee-centre-brewing-emporium/) (bitcoin accepted). Whether you're a total beginner or a seasoned maxi, head along to chat, share ideas, and connect with the local Bitcoin community from 10:00 to 12:00.

####  Saturday

🚶‍♀️[**Bitcoin Walk - London**](https://bitcoinwalk.org/london/): The London Bitcoin Walk will meet this Saturday at the Peter Pan statue in **Hyde Park** at **11:00**. Check the Telegram group chat (available at the link) for details.

🚶🏽‍♂️ [**Bitcoin Walk - Edinburgh**](https://bitcoinwalk.org/edinburgh/): Join fellow bitcoiners every Saturday at **12:00** for a walk, meeting at **Meadows City Tennis Club**. It's a relaxed way to keep fit, enjoy the city, and chat about Bitcoin along the way.
