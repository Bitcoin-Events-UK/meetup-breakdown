### New Businesses Accepting Bitcoin

⛪ **[Christ Church](https://www.christchurchlboro.org/)**: A Reformed Baptist church in Loughborough committed to worshipping the Triune God and serving the local community through faithful Bible teaching, prayer, and gospel witness. Meeting at 25A Herbert Street (LE11 1NU), the church continues a Christian presence established on the site over 100 years ago and holds to the 1689 Baptist Confession of Faith as a summary of its beliefs.

⚙️ [**Bit Fabs**](https://bit-fabs.com/): A Birmingham-based metal fabrication company connecting Bitcoin with real-world engineering through precision manufacturing and bespoke fabrication services. Using sustainably sourced recycled metals, Bit Fabs offers laser cutting, CNC machining, welding, powder coating, bending, and consultancy for everyone from hobbyists to large-scale industry. Built around the idea of turning digital assets into tangible products, they’re pioneering a new model for British manufacturing powered by **Bitcoin** (warning, some shitcoins are mentioned on the website).
